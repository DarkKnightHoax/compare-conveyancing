# Compare the Conveyancing Market — Authority & Outreach Pack

**Prepared:** 31 August 2026  
**Primary resource:** `https://www.comparetheconveyancingmarket.co.uk/uk-conveyancing-cost-guide`  
**Supporting resource:** `https://www.comparetheconveyancingmarket.co.uk/seller-conveyancing-checklist`

## Purpose

The aim is to earn **relevant editorial mentions and citations** from UK property, homebuyer, mortgage and legal-industry audiences. This pack is deliberately designed around useful consumer resources, primary-source references and topical commentary—rather than paid links, link exchanges, repetitive guest posts or other schemes that create little long-term authority.

> The strongest pitch is not “please link to us.” It is: “we have made a useful, accurate resource that helps your readers understand a current home-moving issue, and we can provide an informed, attributable comment if it supports your coverage.”

| Asset | Reader benefit | Best fit |
|---|---|---|

## Editorial and Compliance Rules

| Do | Do not |
|---|---|
| Pitch a relevant, original resource or a timely evidence-led comment. | Pay for unlabelled links, buy links, join private blog networks or use automated directory blasts. |
| Be transparent if an arrangement is sponsored or commercial. | Require a publisher to use a follow link as part of a paid placement. |
| Tailor each pitch to the publication’s audience and current coverage. | Send the same generic guest article to multiple sites. |
| Keep a record of outreach date, recipient, response and published URL. | Make savings, speed, success-rate, rating or market-share claims that cannot be substantiated. |
