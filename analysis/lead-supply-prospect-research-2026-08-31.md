## Required safeguards before any proposal

1. Send only a non-disclosing partnership proposal until individual lead sharing is supported by a separate, specific opt-in and a documented lawful basis.
2. Describe the proposed volume as an indicative future capacity, not an assurance that the historical pool will be sold or transferred.
3. Require any prospective firm to complete due diligence and agree the data-controller roles, permitted purpose, security measures, retention, complaint handling and suppression process before receiving personal data.
4. Do not describe enquiries as "hot" or guarantee conversion rates; all material quality and eligibility claims must be evidenced.
