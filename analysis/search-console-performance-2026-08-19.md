## Initial interpretation

The domain has meaningful brand-query visibility, including positions 5–10 for several variations containing “compare the market”. It does not yet rank competitively for non-brand commercial queries such as “compare conveyancing”, “conveyancing comparison”, “free conveyancing quotes”, or “compare conveyancers”; these sit mainly outside the first five Google result pages. The low click-through rate is consistent with this position mix and with the trademark-adjacent phrase “compare the market”, which can attract users seeking the separate Compare the Market brand rather than conveyancing quotes.

The immediate high-impact opportunity is to expand the currently small indexed footprint (three pages) with focused, genuinely helpful landing pages for the core transactional intent clusters and then improve their internal linking, metadata, and on-page reassurance elements.
