## Editorial constraints

- The public guide should link readers to primary sources for current tax and official fees rather than repeating figures that could become outdated.
- The resource is limited to general information for England and Wales and includes a clear legal/tax-advice disclaimer.
- No customer reviews, testimonial quotations, or unsupported outcome claims are used in the resource.
