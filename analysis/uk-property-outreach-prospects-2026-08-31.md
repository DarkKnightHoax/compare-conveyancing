## Outreach rules

1. Lead with a genuinely useful, original resource—not a request to “add a link.”
2. Cite primary sources and avoid unverified speed, savings, rating, or market-share claims.
3. Do not buy links, use reciprocal-link schemes, or distribute the same generic article at scale.
4. Use only relevant, transparent sponsorship or advertorial arrangements, with appropriate disclosure and `rel="sponsored"` where the publisher determines it is appropriate.
5. Request approval before sending any external outreach email or submitting any paid placement.
